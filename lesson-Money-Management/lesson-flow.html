Lesson level

Teaching order is not folder order. The lesson follows money from coming in to being protected, so Part 3 & 5 comes after Part 6, and Part 8 comes before Part 7. Each part is one segment of about 12 minutes: a Jess moment, a short teach, one core activity, and a line on My Money Plan. Extra worksheets and videos become optional extensions for the third hour.
